    Practice

    Reading about it isn't the same as building it. A few things help it stick:

    • Predict, then reveal. Figures ask for your guess before they show the answer. Being wrong first is the point.
    • Build it. Every lesson's key functions with the bodies removed and the tests left in.
    • In the wild. The same mechanisms in the source of real implementations, verbatim and linked to the exact lines. Open each post's section after its exercises.
    • Review. A few spaced-repetition cards a day from the parts you've read. Each post opens with a warm-up from the one before.

    The code

    Each part has a standalone lesson in clojure/src/lessonNN and go/lessonNN with a runnable demo; the capstone puts everything together. Or skip the install: the terminal runs the Go programs in your browser, compiled to WebAssembly.
